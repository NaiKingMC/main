QUICK REVIEW

Study notes

A few important ideas to remember before the quiz.

01

DNA

DNA stores genetic instructions used by living organisms. In eukaryotic cells, most DNA is found in the nucleus.

02

Nucleic acids

DNA and RNA are nucleic acids. DNA contains deoxyribose sugar, while RNA contains ribose sugar.

03

Central dogma

In the simplified pathway, DNA is transcribed into RNA, and RNA is translated to help build a protein.

04

Cell energy

Mitochondria produce most of the ATP in many eukaryotic cells through cellular respiration.
